# Part 1 — preliminary media research

Status: completed media research for the WWI/WWII narration. Four selected high-resolution photographs and six short documentary clips are local. See [media-sources.md](./media-sources.md) and `media-sources.json` for the final selection, primary records, exact cuts and credits. No composition was changed and no Remotion/Chrome render was run by the sourcing agent.

## Available local archive

Six ready-to-use clips in `public/video` total 52 seconds of media: `qe-arrival`, `qe-bow`, `pow-arrival`, `f35-landing`, `ordnance-lift` (9 seconds each), and `ordnance-deck` (7 seconds). They originate from only four documentary sources, so reusing them alone would not provide enough variety for a new three-minute section.

Full source files, existing contact sheets and cached source pages are present in `out/footage-v5`. Source/author/date/license/probe records are frozen in `data/video-sources.json`. Each of the four sources was recorded as DVIDS PUBLIC DOMAIN; preserve the individual work's attribution and date. Historical illustration must not be presented as footage of a different event or year.

## Final historical selection

The transcript concerns WWI, Jutland, WWII convoys, Force Z, air cover and American industrial expansion. The final selection therefore uses Grand Fleet and Jutland IWM photography, identified1941 Prince of Wales/Repulse photographs, an OSS/NARA Atlantic convoy film, a1943 US Navy shipyard launch and one brief modern Prince of Wales detail for the explicit name comparison. Modern carrier footage is not used to depict historical warfare.
